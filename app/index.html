Roadmap to the platinum

Step 1 — Story & side strands

60–80 hours · 1 playthrough

Play the 5 chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan new vehicles with WAiNK, study animals through the binoculars, and do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house the morning Lucia mentions it. Cheats switch trophies off, so keep them off this save.

Step 2 — 100% checklist & challenges

~100 hours · after the story

The checklist opens under Progress › Stats › 100% Completion. It is forgiving, closer to 60% true completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 of 397 stunts. Robberies, underwater finds, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need everything.

Step 3 — Strangers & cleanup

~10 hours · free roam

Strangers & Freaks strands are not part of the 100% checklist, so finish the 8 that count here, then mop up anything left. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter: 15 manual slots plus 1 autosave, and no mission replay.

Only one trophy may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, disappears after Chapter 1. Everything else can be done in free roam after the story.
